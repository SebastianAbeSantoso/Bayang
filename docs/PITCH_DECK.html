The 10-Minute Shift Change Problem

During rapid afternoon-to-evening shift handovers, baristas leave informal multilingual voice and chat notes while POS systems look spotless.

💬 Outgoing Barista Note (Dimas, 22:02):
"Tadi grinder macet 20 menit pas jam 17, buang beans 200g house blend, es batu abis dari jam 18, gak sempet deep clean steam wand kanan karena antrian rame"

1. Unrecorded Waste & Shrinkage

Baristas purge 200g of espresso beans during a jam without posting an ERP inventory adjustment (INV-20261012-AFT). Stock sheets assume inventory is intact, creating phantom inventory and sudden stockouts.

VIOLATION: RULE R-STK-002

2. Unassigned Equipment Jams

A 20-minute Mahlkönig grinder stoppage is buried in chat. No technician or maintenance ticket is created, leaving the incoming morning crew to inherit a jammed grinder during their peak 7:00 AM rush.

VIOLATION: RULE POL-EQP-004

3. Supply-Chain Delivery Gaps

Ice hit zero at 18:00, but supplier PO-4481 (Arctic Ice Co.) is scheduled for 10:00 AM next morning. Unnoticed, the cafe opens at 7:00 AM with zero ice for 3 hours during iced latte peak.

VIOLATION: RULE R-STK-001
